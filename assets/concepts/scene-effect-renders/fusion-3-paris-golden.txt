PARIS GOLDEN HOUR. Cream limestone frame with delicate black wrought-iron details at the door edges, warm grey-blue back panels, herringbone oak floors, ivory bed with rose-coral throw, a vintage-modern desk with a compact terminal and books, a tiny window-box of geraniums. Outside: stepped Haussmann rooftops with zinc grey mansards and chimney pots as terraces; distance soft gold light: the Eiffel Tower, Seine bridge, Sacré-Cœur on its hill, La Défense small and pale. Daylight golden, not night.
