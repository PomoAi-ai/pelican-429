TOKYO CLEAR WINTER DAY. White frame with light hinoki wood trims, pale grey back panels, low wooden furniture, shoji-like light panels at the doorway, neat tea corner, a small round companion robot. Outside: stepped garden terraces with clipped pines and a small stone lantern; distance crisp and pale: the orange-white Tokyo Tower, dense mid-rise city, an elevated train line, the Skytree spire and snow-capped Mt Fuji on a clear blue horizon.
