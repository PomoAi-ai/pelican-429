=== 共用约束（每条提示词末尾都附加）

Constraints:
- Subject is ONLY the city skyline as a wide background plate: no foreground objects, no people, no characters, no text, no letters, no logos, no signage, no watermark, no UI.
- The city itself forms the horizon: no mountains, no hills, no cliffs, no waterfalls.
- Every tower is a distinct design (height, silhouette, roof, facade pattern); no repeated or copy-pasted buildings, no tiled patterns.
- Neutral, natural white balance and true material colors: no overall yellow/orange cast and no overall blue cast; color comes from the light and materials described above.
- Deep depth of field, everything sharp from near to far, no bokeh, no tilt-shift blur.
- Wide 16:9 landscape composition, horizon roughly at the lower third, clear focal point.
